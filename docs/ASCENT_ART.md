# Ascent scene art

The mountain backdrop, close ice wall and four climbing poses are original generated illustrations, not photographs or claims of topographic accuracy. They evoke Everest and the surrounding Himalaya. The route, camera, parallax, pose timing and day/night treatment remain editable SVG/CSS/JavaScript. All art was generated with the built-in imagegen tool, not a fallback CLI.

- Generation mode: built-in imagegen tool, `stylized-concept` use case for the background and transparent assets for the wall/climber.
- Original: `assets/img/ascent-everest-illustration.png` (1086 × 1448).
- Optimized background sources: `assets/media/ascent-everest-illustration.webp` (1086 px), `ascent-everest-illustration-800.webp`, `ascent-everest-illustration-640.webp`, and `ascent-everest-illustration-640.avif`.
- The mobile AVIF source is deliberately smaller to keep the large scene from delaying first paint. The site serves responsive formats from `<picture>`; no external image service is used.
- Foreground source: `assets/img/ascent-icewall-illustration.png`; responsive alpha WebP: `assets/media/ascent-icewall-800.webp` and `ascent-icewall-480.webp`, with `ascent-icewall-480.avif` on supported mobile browsers.
- Climber source: `assets/img/ascent-climber-frames.png`; four transparent animation frames: `assets/media/ascent-climber-01.webp` through `ascent-climber-04.webp`. The four equal source panels were cropped and resized with Pillow; stray edge pixels from adjacent panels were cleared. The first pose loads immediately; later poses are deferred until after load or the first wheel input. Scroll progress chooses among the consistent full-body poses.

Background prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original background illustration for the sticky mountain scene of a premium personal cybersecurity portfolio. Create a vertically composed cinematic illustrated-realism view of Mount Everest and surrounding Himalayan massifs, seen from a steep snowy approach: Everest's distinctive high pyramidal summit and dark exposed rock bands far above, layered glacier-carved ridges, wind-scoured ice, deep crevasses and atmospheric cloud depth. Foreground should have a believable diagonal slope of snow and ice with dark stone protruding, a visually climbable surface around the middle-right of the image. Use sophisticated charcoal, midnight navy, glacier blue and icy white colors, restrained contrast and natural diffuse alpine light, hand-painted / high-end 3D concept-art depth, realistic texture, subtle snow spindrift. Vertical 3:4 composition that can crop responsively. No person, no route line, no tents, no typography, no symbols, no interface, no logo, no watermark. Avoid flat vector geometry, simple triangular infographic mountains, and fantastical forms.

Climber sprite prompt:

> Create a production-ready four-frame animation sprite sheet of ONE consistent human alpine mountaineer, illustrated in high-end painterly realism / 3D concept-art style to match a cinematic snowy Everest portfolio backdrop. Transparent background. The climber is shown from the rear three-quarter angle, facing and climbing toward the RIGHT, wearing a clearly visible bright burnt-orange down parka with quilted baffling, navy technical pants, pale helmet, protective goggles, climbing harness with rope, small slate-blue backpack, ice tool, heavy gloves and crampon boots. Natural adult human anatomy and believable mountaineering technique. Arrange exactly FOUR equally-sized full-body frames in one HORIZONTAL row, with generous transparent margin around each and identical camera, character scale, gear details, perspective and lighting across all four. Frame 1: right hand reaches to a higher hold, left hand anchored, right knee raised. Frame 2: right hand grips and pulls, left hand begins reaching, right foot plants. Frame 3: left hand reaches higher, right hand anchored, left knee raised. Frame 4: left hand grips and pulls, right hand begins reaching, left foot plants. Weight stays close to the wall; torso inclines right with believable balance. Crisp silhouette, nuanced but legible colors, soft cool side light, grounded boots. No wall, rock, mountain, snow surface, route line, shadow, UI, text, numbers, frame outlines, extra people, extra limbs, duplicated equipment, logos or watermark. Transparent alpha around each full figure. This is a sprite sheet, not a comic strip.

Foreground wall prompt:

> Create a single tall transparent-background foreground environmental illustration for a premium Everest expedition website. Subject ONLY: a close-up steep Himalayan ice-and-rock climbing wall, seen in three-quarter perspective from slightly behind the climber, extending vertically beyond the canvas. The wall enters from the RIGHT edge and occupies roughly the rightmost 45% of a 3:4 vertical composition; keep the LEFT 55% fully transparent so a separate mountaineer figure can be composited there. The wall has an irregular stepped LEFT silhouette near the middle-right, with believable small snow-covered ledges and ice-tool placements approximately every 20% of the height. Strong depth: dark slate rock recesses, thick translucent glacier-blue ice ribs, crisp icy overhangs, snow cornices, glancing cool side light, contact shadows and atmospheric haze. The wall should feel textured, volumetric, mountaineer-scale and physically climbable, NOT a distant mountain panorama, not flat vector, not a plain geometric polygon. Palette charcoal navy, teal glacial blue, icy off-white, restrained cinematic illustrated realism matching a snowy Himalayan massif. Transparent alpha everywhere left of the irregular wall silhouette, no sky or mountain background. No person, gear, rope, route line, text, interface, labels, logos or watermark.
