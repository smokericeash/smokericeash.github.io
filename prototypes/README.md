# Contact-anchored Ascent implementation

The live `/journey/` page loads `assets/js/ice-climber-rig.js` and `assets/js/photo-climber-rig.js` before `assets/js/expedition.js`. They are copies of the authoring sources `ice-climber-rig.js` and `photo-leg-rig.js` here. The earlier fourteen complete-pose drawings remain intact and can be compared with `?rig=legacy`; they also serve as the no-JavaScript and reduced-motion fallback. The site layout, milestones, mountain vista, and editorial content were preserved.

The solver evaluates permanent left and right anatomical limbs in shared wall coordinates. Planted tool and boot contacts remain fixed during each step, and reverse scroll re-evaluates the same deterministic pose. SVG groups carry transparent photographic jacket and trouser crops. The climb retains the rear/right-facing black-and-white character with orange tools and boots. Near the end, the wall gives way to a summit ledge and the character plants a flag before a standing pose. Scroll distance derives from the milestone count.

The scoped appearance correction keeps every solved pose identical to `aad3cb3`. The existing leg skin is broader, a clipped photographic waist from the summit portrait bridges the jacket and thigh roots, and clipped rope coils from the preserved original climber follow the waist gear. The far tool paints behind the body. The final flag uses clipped pixels from the original planting image, with its transform matched to that image, so its dark-blue fabric remains the same after the standing pose appears. None of the approved complete-pose image files changed.

Transparent art sources: `climber-rig-components-study.png`, `ascent-planting-pose-study.png`, `ascent-standing-pose-study.png`, and `ascent-summit-ledge-study.png`. Built-in ImageGen generated these from the approved character and mountain references, with the same rear/right perspective and gear, a planted flag, hands-on-hips standing pose, and an empty snowy ridge extension. The precise prompt text for the first three images was not retained in the previous handoff. The ledge prompt was: “Create a transparent summit extension from the existing glacier-and-rock wall illustration, preserving the close wall's snowy ice and dark rock, with a natural upper snow ridge/ledge on the right and transparent open mountain vista on the left; no person, no flag.” `export-rig-assets.cjs` exports the transparent WebP assets used by the page.

With the local production preview running at `http://127.0.0.1:4173/`, verify using `bundle exec jekyll build`, `node prototypes/rig-physics.cjs`, `node prototypes/real-layout-preview.cjs`, and `npm run test:contact-rig`. The physics check samples 1,001 progress positions for reach, planted-contact drift, anatomical identity, and visible-joint continuity. The browser check samples real wheel-driven motion, reverse scrubbing, mobile overflow, reduced motion, art loading, and the summit. The layout preview writes ignored desktop/mobile captures in `test-results/`. `probe-summit.cjs` prints the climber/planting/standing visibility across the final scroll stage for browser-specific diagnosis. `scan-wall.cjs` reports the source cutout's alpha silhouette for further contact calibration.

`node prototypes/appearance-review.cjs` also compares 1,001 complete pose states against approved commit `aad3cb3`, checks torso-to-boot alpha connectivity at 32 points through the alternating cycle, writes enlarged phase images to `test-results/`, and compares the dark-blue cloth pixels before and after the final portrait handoff. The hand releasing the pole is intentionally excluded from the cloth comparison.

The contact curve is hand-tuned; the source wall's alpha edge varies greatly with height and is not a foothold map. The final poses use brief opaque handovers so a slow scrub never shows a double silhouette; the pose change may still be perceptible at the exact handover. Real-device wheel/touch review, remote Linux CI, and publication-domain review remain before production release. Do not push or merge into `main` until those checks are complete.
